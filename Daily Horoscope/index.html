Common Questions

How do I choose my Rashi?

Select your known birth Moon sign. In Vedic astrology, it is not determined by Western Sun-sign date ranges.

Are these live daily predictions?

No. This staging interface displays illustrative readings. The readings are illustrative; astronomical calculations are separate and not independently validated.

Which languages are available?

English, Marathi and Hindi. Use the language buttons in the header.
